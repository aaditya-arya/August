# Write — 1-Click Google Keep Chrome Extension Scraper

Zero-friction, 1-click import from Google Keep directly into your **Write** brain graph without downloading Google Takeout or copy-pasting.

---

### How to Load in Chrome (15 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome** (or Brave / Edge / Arc).
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions`.
3. In the top-right corner, toggle **Developer mode** to **ON**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left button).
5. Select the `extension` folder located inside your project directory:
   `c:\Users\DELL\OneDrive\Desktop\P\potvt\extension`
6. Pin the **Write - Google Keep Sync** extension to your Chrome toolbar.

---

### How to Use

1. Open your notes on [**keep.google.com**](https://keep.google.com).
2. Click the **Write** extension icon in your Chrome toolbar.
3. Click **⚡ Scrape & Sync All Keep Notes**.
4. The extension reads all note cards, titles, and checklists from your screen and streams them directly into Write!
